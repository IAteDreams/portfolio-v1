Live AI Face-Swap Streaming

A platform where streaming agencies run their talent with real-time AI face swap applied straight into the browser broadcast. Three roles — platform owner, agency, streamer — each with their own console: metered subscription plans in ¥, live session monitoring, shared face libraries, and full audit trails, localized JA/EN.

client project — built for agencies managing live-streaming talent at scale
✳
// the streamer studio

Pick a face, go live.

Streamers choose a saved face and background, set stream quality, and start a session — the swap runs in real time over WebRTC with live face-lock and face-match quality metrics. Usage counts down against the agency's plan hours.

✺
// the agency workspace

Run the talent, watch the meter.

Agencies manage streamers and managers within plan limits, share faces and backgrounds across the team, monitor live sessions and service health, and handle their own subscription — with overtime billed per hour past the plan.

✳
// the owner console

The whole platform, one seat.

The platform owner onboards agencies and delegate operators, creates and re-prices Stripe-backed plans, reconciles revenue — MRR, ARPU, churn, plan distribution — and audits every admin action.

✦
// what I owned

Built end to end.

Real-time swap pipeline

AKOOL face swap over Agora WebRTC, applied live into the broadcast with face-lock / face-match quality telemetry and provider failover checks.

Three-role architecture

Owner, agency, and streamer consoles on one codebase — scoped permissions, invite-based onboarding, and delegate operator accounts.

Metered billing

Stripe-backed plan tiers metered on concurrent sessions, streaming hours, faces and seats — with per-hour overtime past plan limits.

Revenue reporting

MRR/ARR, ARPU, churn, plan distribution, and a 12-month trend split subscription vs overage — with reconciliation against the ledger.

Teams & shared libraries

Plan-gated streamer and manager seats with CSV bulk import, and face / background libraries shared agency-wide.

Ops, audit & localization

Service health checks across AKOOL, Agora, GPU and credits; a full audit trail of admin actions; the whole product localized JA/EN.
