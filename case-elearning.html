Gamified E-Learning Platform

A Udemy-style AI-design school built end to end at MabuhayTech: video courses and programs, an XP / badge / streak gamification engine with a live leaderboard, a freelance job board, subscriptions with a referral payout program — and the admin panel that runs all of it, localized for the Japanese market.

Learn, level up, get hired.

// gamification & community

XP, badges, and real work.

Every lesson feeds an XP economy — titles, badges, and streaks — surfaced on a podium leaderboard. Graduates apply to real freelance projects from a built-in job board, and a referral program pays out monthly commissions.

✳
// the admin panel

Seventeen modules, one panel.

Users, content, mentoring, submissions, affiliates, subscriptions, support tickets, course analytics, badges, marketing — plus plan-distribution and MRR reporting, all from a single operations panel.

// what I owned

Built end to end.

Course engine

Programs → courses → modules → video lessons, with progress tracking, resume-learning, and per-genre analytics.

Gamification system

XP economy with earned titles, unlockable badges, daily streaks, milestones, and a podium leaderboard across 100+ users.

Subscriptions & billing

Free and paid tiers with plan management, past-due detection, plan-distribution reporting, and MRR estimates.

Referral payouts

Click → signup → conversion tracking with pending / confirmed / paid commission states and threshold-based monthly payouts.

Freelance job board

Project listings with budgets and terms, bookmarks, applications, and an admin review pipeline for submissions.

Admin & localization

A 17-module operations panel with support tickets and marketing tools — the whole product localized JA/EN.
